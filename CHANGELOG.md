# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-17

First public release.

- alert when the camped enemy player resurrects: raid warning sound 3x on the Master channel, on-screen warning, taskbar flash
- auto-camp a dead enemy player on your target, or camp by name with `/ccamp Name`
- detection through target, focus, mouseover and nameplates, since Forever closes the combat log to addons
- minimap button and camp panel with live state and timer
- key binding that targets the camped player
- `/ccamp setup` for nameplate settings
- CampProbe, an optional diagnostic addon
